## What's New in This Update

This update turns Techguns Expanded into a full **crafting expansion for Techguns' weapons and armor**. On top of the existing power and materials system, you can now build a whole line of Techguns gear from scratch — including two complete high-tier armor sets — through the machines and workbench you already use.

### Craftable weapons

Three Techguns weapons can now be crafted instead of only found:

- **The Infiltrator** — the silenced M4 variant, built from an M4 Assault Rifle on the crafting table.
- **Alien Blaster** — assembled on the crafting table from laser and high-tech components.
- **Blaster Shotgun** — fabricated from a Laser Rifle in the Fabricator.

### Two complete armor sets

Full craft trees for both of Techguns' top armor tiers — helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots for each:

- **Nether Combat armor** (the Praetor set)
- **Power Armor Mk2**

Both sets are built from a new chain of intermediate materials, so getting a full suit is a real progression goal rather than a lucky drop.

### New materials and components

Eight new items were added to support the weapon and armor recipes:

- **Nether Armor Plating** and **Power Armor Plating Mk2** — the armor plating for each set
- **Advanced Cybernetic Parts** and **Elite Cybernetic Parts** — tiered electronics
- **Tungsten Carbide Plate** — pressed from Tungsten Carbide Ingots
- **Mechanical Parts (Titan)** and **Mechanical Parts (Tungsten Carbide)** — machined gears
- **Overheated Blaze Rod** — a superheated crafting reagent

### New machine processing

The new materials tie into Techguns' own machines, giving each a purpose in the tech tree:

- **Metal Press** — presses ingots into plates and machines gears
- **Fabricator** — assembles the armor plating and the higher-tier cybernetics
- **Blast Furnace** — forges the Overheated Blaze Rod from a Magma Block and a Blaze Rod

### Fixes

- Placed Oil can now be picked back up with an empty bucket in survival, like any other fluid.
- Fixed several item textures that failed to load in-game.
